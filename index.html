ALGORITHM · CALIBRATION

top-up fill (fuel already in tank):
  currentFuel      = tankSize × (gaugeValue / 20)
  available        = tankSize − currentFuel
  currentEthanol   = currentFuel × currentE
  highToAdd        = (tankSize × targetE − currentEthanol − available × baseE) / (highE − baseE)
  baseToAdd        = available − highToAdd
  finalE           = (currentEthanol + highToAdd × highE + baseToAdd × baseE) / tankSize

full-tank blend:
  highGallons      = tankSize × (targetE − currentE) / (highE − baseE)
  baseGallons      = tankSize − highGallons

octane:
  AKI              = volume-weighted mean of fuel AKI values
  RON (estimate)   = AKI + offset        (linear model, not a lab measurement)

units: 1 US gal = 3.785411784 L (exact) · validity tolerance 1e-7 · gauge 0–20, step 0.5
